Expert Knowledge

Fabric Care Guidelines & Advisory

Essential care advisories curated by our master garment preservationists.

Never store traditional embroidered garments inside plastic wrappers long-term, as synthetic films trap humidity and accelerate metallic thread oxidation. Always store in breathable cotton dust bags on wide wooden hangers or folded flat with acid-free tissue paper between embroidered segments.
Conventional cleaners rely heavily on perchloroethylene (perc), an aggressive solvent that strips natural lanolin from wool and causes silk fibers to turn brittle over time. Aisance utilizes pure hydrocarbon extraction combined with botanical conditioners that clean thoroughly while retaining natural fiber elasticity and luster.
Our standard atelier turnaround is 48 hours. For high-priority executive suites or urgent ceremonial timelines, we offer a dedicated 24-hour Express Valet service upon request.
