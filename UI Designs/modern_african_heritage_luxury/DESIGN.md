## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an aura of sovereign stability, quiet affluence, and multi-generational stewardship suited for a top-tier Nigerian diversified service conglomerate. It reflects the intersection of classical continental luxury and contemporary African institutional gravitas.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Precision**:
- **Dignified Restraint:** Expansive warm ivory negative space mimics archival editorial spreads rather than utilitarian SaaS dashboards.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Asymmetric, disciplined layouts rooted in structural editorial grids convey timeless institutional weight.
- **Subtle Organic Texture:** Subtle botanical geometry and leafy filigree inspired by classical monograms appear strictly as watermark-level background motifs, hairline embossing, or deliberate vector accents—never as ornamental clutter.
- **Absolute Avoidance:** Neon accents, aggressive gradient glassmorphism, over-rounded toy-like surfaces, and hyper-saturated hues are strictly prohibited.

## Colors
The palette balances institutional authority with quiet opulence:

- **Primary (`#12372A` - Deep Forest Green):** The sovereign anchor. Applied to foundational containers, hero backgrounds, primary text headings, and formal brand framing.
- **Secondary (`#C9A24A` - Champagne Gold):** The prestige signifier. Reserved for high-value interactive calls-to-action, executive key-line highlights, active states, and decorative hairline dividers. Avoid large background fills of gold; use it as an illuminating highlight.
- **Tertiary (`#DDE7E1` - Sage Green):** The resting surface tint. Employed for secondary cards, pill tag backgrounds, delicate borders, and low-contrast UI backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#171717` - Charcoal):** The reading standard. Guarantees uncompromising accessibility and optical clarity across body copy, tabular data, and legal disclosures.
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F5EF` - Warm Ivory):** The structural canvas. Replaces clinical digital white with an unbleached linen and archival paper warmth.

## Typography
Typographic scale balances classical editorial elegance with crisp modern structure:

- **EB Garamond (Display & Headings):** Conveys intellectual authority, historical continuity, and bespoke craftsmanship. Used for primary narrative moments, executive statements, and financial milestones. Always set with generous line heights and subtle negative tracking on large scales.
- **Montserrat (Body & UI):** Provides geometric order, readability, and structural efficiency. Used across body paragraphs, tables, form inputs, and system navigation.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** Labels and section tags are set in Montserrat uppercase with tracked letterforms (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to maintain structural rhythm against the serif headings.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column editorial grid on desktop (max-width: 1440px), shifting to an 8-column grid on tablet and a 4-column grid on mobile:

- **Canvas Cadence:** Generous outer margins (`4rem` desktop) preserve breathing room, reinforcing the physical weight of an upscale publication.
- **Structural Gutters:** Desktop gutters (`1.5rem`) separate complex portfolio verticals, operational data, and subsidiary overviews cleanly.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Content sections must not crowd one another; use major spacing multipliers (`space-xl` and above) between unrelated thematic blocks to maintain an unhurried, executive pacing.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is established primarily through **Tonal Layering** and **Hairline Outlines**, rather than simulated 3D drop shadows:

- **Flat Precision:** Surfaces rest directly on Warm Ivory (`#F7F5EF`), distinguishing themselves via borders colored with Champagne Gold at 30% opacity or Sage Green (`#DDE7E1`).
- **Surface Tiering:** Elevated layers (e.g., dropdowns, executive modal drawers) leverage a subtle tonal shift to `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px solid `#DDE7E1` boundary.
- **Ambient Floor Shadow:** Where floating elevation is mandatory (such as navigation bars or priority popovers), rely strictly on an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(18, 55, 42, 0.08)`. The tinted green undertone eliminates dirty gray artifacts and harmonizes with the brand palette.

## Shapes
A "Soft" roundedness (`0.25rem` standard, `0.5rem` for large containers) preserves an architectural, authoritative profile:

- Avoid pill shapes or bubbly radius tokens. The silhouette remains sharp and structured to evoke stationery, classical architecture, and high-end print collateral.
- Visual anchors (featured subsidiary portfolio panels, balance sheets, hero cards) use subtle `0.25rem` corner breaks with 1px structural framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Deep Forest Green (`#12372A`), label in Warm Ivory (`#F7F5EF`) using Montserrat SemiBold uppercase with `0.06em` letter spacing. 1px border of `#12372A`. Hover transitions smoothly to a subtle Champagne Gold accent border or an inner tone of `#1a4a39`.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Background in transparent Warm Ivory with a 1px solid Champagne Gold (`#C9A24A`) border and text in Deep Forest Green (`#12372A`).
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Uncontained text button in Deep Forest Green with an animated 1px underline rendered in Champagne Gold.

### Cards
- **Portfolio & Subsidiary Cards:** Built on Warm Ivory or white surfaces, bordered with a delicate 1px line of Sage Green (`#DDE7E1`). Optional watermark of the botanical leaf motif positioned in the bottom-right corner at 5% opacity.
- **Interactive State:** Hover lifts the card by 2px, shifting the border to Champagne Gold (`#C9A24A`) with the ambient green shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Subsidiary Indicators:** Compact, structured tags with a `0.25rem` border-radius. Background in Sage Green (`#DDE7E1`) with text in Deep Forest Green (`#12372A`).
- **Premium/Executive Tags:** Deep Forest Green background with Champagne Gold typography and an optional hairline gold border.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Fields:** Subtle Sage Green or Warm Ivory background with a crisp bottom border or full 1px boundary in `#DDE7E1`. Text in Charcoal (`#171717`). On focus, the field transitions to a 1px Champagne Gold (`#C9A24A`) border with zero outer glow.
- **Labels:** Montserrat Medium, uppercase tracking, positioned above the field for crisp corporate readability.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with a minute `2px` radius (or classic circular radio) framed by a 1px `#12372A` stroke. Checked state fills with Deep Forest Green (`#12372A`) revealing a Champagne Gold (`#C9A24A`) checkmark or central indicator.

### Dividers & Botanical Accents
- Horizontal rules must utilize Champagne Gold at 25% opacity or Sage Green at 50% opacity.
- Structural section dividers may feature a centered, minimalist botanical leaf icon glyph rendered in hairline Champagne Gold.
